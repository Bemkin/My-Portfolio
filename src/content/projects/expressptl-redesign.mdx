### 🚛 Express Transport & Logistics (Express PTL) — Platform Modernization

**Express Transport & Logistics (Express PTL)** is one of East Africa's most critical multimodal freight carriers, operating continuously since 2013 with 76 company-owned heavy prime movers, 2,916 metric tons of synchronous lift capacity, and Authorized Economic Operator (AEO) certified customs clearance across Ethiopia and the Horn of Africa.

This project represents a complete digital transformation and engineering redesign, turning a conventional corporate site into an immersive, high-authority web platform that demonstrates operational dominance and streamlines multi-ton freight procurement.

#### 1. Algorithmic Freight Quote Calculator
- **Multi-Factor Dispatch Estimator**: Enables enterprise logistics directors and humanitarian aid coordinators (e.g. UN WFP) to calculate estimated transit durations, fleet requirements, and tariff tiers on the fly.
- **Dynamic Variable Matrices**: Calibrates point of origin (Port of Djibouti / Doraleh DCT, Berbera, Moyale), destination dry ports (Addis Ababa Central Terminal, Mojo Dry Port, Hawassa, Kombolcha), cargo classifications (Containerized FCL, Heavy Machinery >40 MT, Bulk Agriculture), and bonded AEO customs clearance.
- **Instant Allocation Breakdown**: Calculates transit windows (3–5 days standard corridor) and dynamically assigns required equipment configurations (Standard Prime Mover Flatbed, Goldhofer Multi-Axle Hydraulic Lowbeds).

#### 2. Cinematic Sticky Dual-Video Ambient Canvas
- **Seamless Preloader-to-Hero Expansion**: Implements a synchronized intro loading sequence with percentage counter tracking and session storage caching (`sessionStorage.getItem('express_ptl_preloader_seen')`) to ensure first-time visitors experience an impactful cinematic curtain reveal without disrupting return visits.
- **Dual 100vh Sticky Viewport**: Employs sticky background video canvases that pin behind scrolling section layers with ambient vignette overlays, preventing jarring layout jumps and maintaining fluid visual continuity between the Hero, About, Advantages, and Partners sections.

#### 3. Industrial High-Craft Typography & Micro-Interactions
- **Font Stack**: Custom type pairing utilizing **Bebas Neue** for bold industrial headline authority, **Barlow Condensed** for technical readouts, **Outfit** for clean navigational elements, and **Plus Jakarta Sans** for body legibility.
- **Reading Scrub Text**: Custom viewport-scrubbing text reveals that dynamically illuminate individual words as the user scrolls, driving intense narrative focus.
- **Lenis Inertia Scrolling**: Hardware-accelerated 60 FPS inertia smooth scrolling configured with responsive resize observers and modal scroll-lock coordination.

#### 4. Multimodal Fleet & Regional Corridor Infrastructure
- **76 Heavy Prime Movers**: Interactive fleet catalog featuring technical specifications for Volvo FH16 6×4 and Mercedes-Benz Actros heavy haulage tractor units.
- **Specialized Heavy Haulage**: Deep breakdown of Goldhofer modular hydraulic multi-axle lowbeds, extendable flatbeds, and high-capacity reinforced sideboard trailers.
- **Corridor Showcase**: Full strategic mapping of the 910 km Djibouti–Addis Ababa economic lifeline, Bole Bulbula operations depot, and regional dry port connectivity.
