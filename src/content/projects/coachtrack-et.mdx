### ⚽ CoachTrack ET — Pitch-Side Telegram Mini App (TMA)

**CoachTrack ET** transforms grassroots sports management by bringing live stopwatch tracking, timesheet aggregation, and automated local currency billing into the app coaches already keep open: **Telegram**.

---

### 🌟 Key Architecture & Highlights

1. **Pitch-Side Live Stopwatch with State Resilience**:
   - 1-tap `Start Practice` locks in kickoff timestamp.
   - Built with persistent local timer state: even if the coach takes an incoming phone call, leaves the app, or restarts Telegram, the stopwatch continues seamlessly without losing elapsed training time.
   - 1-tap `Finish & Log` auto-calculates exact decimal training hours (e.g. `14:20` to `17:50` = `3.50 hrs`).

2. **1-Tap Weather & Rain Cancellation**:
   - Records a `NO PRACTICE` session flag with reasons (Rain, Pitch Flooded, Public Holiday).
   - Sets 0 hours without distorting cumulative billing calculations or spreadsheet integrity.

3. **Automated ETB Financials & Parent Invoicing**:
   - Calculates total dues on the fly: `Completed Sessions × Attendance × Rate/Kid/Session` (e.g. `6 × 4 × 1,000 ETB = 24,000 ETB`).
   - Generates verified statements with Ethiopian payment methods:
     - **Telebirr** (with 1-click mobile copy)
     - **Commercial Bank of Ethiopia (CBE)** account numbers
   - 1-tap **"Send to Parent Telegram Group"** outputs a cleanly formatted invoice ready for group communication.

4. **Real-Time Google Sheets Sync**:
   - Webhook bridge built with **Google Apps Script** (`google_sheets_sync.gs`).
   - Appends verified rows into the coach's remote Google Sheet the moment a session ends.
