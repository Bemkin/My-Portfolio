### How it works

The pipeline automates the busywork of a targeted job search while keeping a person in charge of every email that goes out.

```
┌──────────────────┐     ┌───────────────────────┐     ┌──────────────────────┐
│ Job sources      │ ──▶ │ RocketReach v2        │ ──▶ │ Google Gemini        │
│ RemoteOK API     │     │ Hiring team → Founder │     │ 3-sentence pitch     │
│ LinkedIn (PW)    │     │ → CTO → VP Eng        │     │ per role track       │
└──────────────────┘     └───────────────────────┘     └──────────┬───────────┘
                                                                  │
                                                                  ▼
┌──────────────────┐     ┌───────────────────────┐     ┌──────────────────────┐
│ Gmail / Apps     │ ◀── │ Telegram bot daemon   │ ◀── │ Draft card           │
│ Script relay +CV │     │ (owner chat only)     │     │ [Send] [Edit] [Skip] │
└──────────────────┘     └───────────────────────┘     └──────────────────────┘
```

### 1. Sourcing

- **RemoteOK** listings come from its public API; **LinkedIn** recommended jobs are collected with a Playwright browser session that reuses a saved login.
- Listings are matched against a configurable keyword list (Agentic AI, TypeScript, Next.js, Supabase, PostgreSQL and more) and checked against a local store of jobs already seen, so nothing is processed twice.
- The pipeline runs once a day on a scheduled task.

### 2. Finding the right person

- When LinkedIn shows a "Meet the hiring team" member, that person is looked up first.
- Otherwise RocketReach is searched by title priority — Founder/Co-Founder, CTO, VP Engineering, then Head of Engineering — with rate limiting and retry handling for credit and throttling errors.

### 3. Protecting sender reputation

- Free webmail addresses (Gmail, Outlook, iCloud and others) are rejected.
- The email domain must match the company, which filters out stale addresses from previous employers.
- RocketReach's own validity status is preferred, and NeverBounce or Verimail can verify the mailbox right before sending.

### 4. Drafting the pitch

- Each role is classified into one of three tracks: **Full-Stack & Frontend**, **Backend & Systems** or **Founding & Applied AI**.
- Gemini receives the job description, the contact and the matching persona, and returns a 3-sentence pitch with a low-friction call to action — or skips the role if it is not remote or not technical.

### 5. Human-in-the-loop dispatch

- Every draft arrives in Telegram with **Send**, **Edit** and **Skip** buttons. Replying to a card rewrites the draft before it is sent.
- Send delivers the email from Gmail with the track's PDF resume attached. On hosts that block SMTP, a Google Apps Script HTTPS relay sends it instead.
- `/status` reports health and `/testemail` sends a test email with the resume attached.

### Engineering notes

- **Security:** the bot ignores every chat except its owner's, cloud draft sync requires a shared secret, and the resume attached to an email is chosen from the profile by track — never from data in the incoming draft.
- **Deployment:** the bot daemon is containerised with Docker and runs on Render's free tier.
- **Quality:** a Vitest suite covers email validation, profile loading and resume attachments, and GitHub Actions runs type-checking and tests on every push.
- **Open source:** personal data lives in a gitignored profile folder, with example profiles in the repo, under an MIT licence.
