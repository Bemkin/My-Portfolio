## The Problem: Improper Form

In fitness, form is everything. Poor technique stalls progress and raises the risk of injury, and most people training alone have no one to tell them what is going wrong. FormCheck AI is my attempt at a virtual coach for the basics.

### Pose Tracking in the Browser

FormCheck runs MediaPipe Pose directly in the browser and draws a skeleton over the camera feed. Each frame goes to an exercise-specific analyser — squat, plank, push-up, lunge and deadlift — that checks joint angles against target ranges, for example knee angle and depth on a squat, and turns them into a score and a correction.

### Adding a Trained Model

Rules only go so far, so for squats I also trained a small RandomForest classifier on MediaPipe keypoints. A Python FastAPI backend streams frames over a WebSocket, combines the classifier's confidence with the rule score, and flags reps that look wrong.

### Two Ways to Review a Workout

Besides the live camera, you can upload a video. The backend sends it to Gemini 2.5 Flash for a structured technique review, with keyframe and OpenCV fallbacks if that fails. Both the WebSocket and video analysis currently run against a locally hosted backend, so the public demo shows the browser side only.

### What I Learned

The app started from a Lovable scaffold and grew into a React and Vite frontend with a Python backend, English and Amharic translations, and Supabase for accounts. The biggest lesson was that pose estimation is the easy part — deciding what counts as "good form" for each exercise is where the real work is.
