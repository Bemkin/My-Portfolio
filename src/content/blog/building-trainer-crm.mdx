## Solving for Solopreneurs

Personal trainers juggle workout plans, appointments, invoices and client messages across notebooks and chat apps. My goal with Trainer CRM was to put all of that in one place, on the web and on a phone.

### One Backend, Two Apps

The web app is React with TypeScript and Vite, and the mobile app is built with Expo and React Native. Both talk to the same Supabase Postgres backend, so a trainer can plan a program at a desk and check it at the gym. Each trainer works inside an organisation, and row-level security on every CRM table keeps one business's clients invisible to another.

### The Day-to-Day Tools

Trainers can keep a client list, build workout programs from ordered exercises (pulled from the ExerciseDB API), schedule appointments, and issue printable invoices with payments recorded against them. A one-click action sends appointment reminders by email through Resend or by SMS through Twilio, and a dashboard charts revenue and client growth.

### Localised for Ethiopia

One feature I care about is localisation. The whole interface is available in English and Amharic, and dates can be picked in the Ethiopian calendar, so trainers can run their business in the language and calendar they actually use.

### What's Next

Some admin work is still manual — reminders are sent with a click rather than on a schedule, and payments are recorded by hand. Automating those is the next step toward giving trainers more time for their clients.
